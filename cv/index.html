Profile

I am a problem solver and user behavior architect focused on how people think, act and adapt within systems. I notice what isn't working for people, define the real problem, and build practical solutions: products, prototypes, learning programs and the way an organisation runs. I have founded and led a team from scratch, designed AI curricula and trained teachers to use AI, and developed a company's organisational structure and standardised ways of measuring its goals. I work across engineering, design and education, and across cultures in English and Mandarin, turning complex ideas into things people can use.

I am looking for user behavior research roles: analysing how people use products, platforms and AI systems, finding where a design works against the user, and proposing changes that serve them. Open to remote work and relocation. References available on request.
